About Me

Where artificial intelligence, compliance and data protection meet.

FIDELIS ST. HILL is an AI Analyst, GRC professional, and Qualified Data Protection Officer with more than 20 years of experience in compliance and project management.

Her professional journey began in traditional compliance and project management, building a strong foundation in regulatory awareness, governance, documentation, risk management and organizational accountability. Since 2025, she has expanded this foundation into artificial intelligence, AI governance, data protection, cybersecurity and technology risk through her work with Cynat Global.

Today, Fidelis focuses on the intersection of AI, Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), and Data Protection. Her work includes developing compliance templates, establishing GRC benchmarks for SaaS and AI applications, building expertise in international AI and data-protection regulations and frameworks, and exploring security frameworks and cloud environments, including AWS.

Her commitment to responsible innovation extends beyond organizational compliance. In 2026, she served as an Agentic Judge for the Caribbean SEED Summit and as an Advisor for the Future Caribbean Buildathon, contributing to the development and evaluation of emerging AI solutions within the Caribbean innovation ecosystem.
